Shadoku Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Shadoku (Android application ID com.group_a1.shadoku) handles information when you use the game.

Information the app uses

Shadoku is designed to work offline and does not require an account. The current app does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, precise location, or payment information. It does not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.

Game data stored on your device

To provide game features, Shadoku stores gameplay information locally in the app's private storage. This can include saved Sudoku puzzles and moves, difficulty statistics, completion times, daily puzzle progress and streaks, Shadow challenge codes and progression, theme selections, and other game preferences.

Shadoku does not operate its own server to receive this gameplay information, and the current app does not intentionally transmit it to the developer or third parties.

Android system backups

Depending on your Android device and backup settings, the operating system may back up or transfer eligible Shadoku application data using Android's system backup and device-transfer features. These services are controlled by your device and its platform provider, not by a Shadoku account or server. You can review your device's backup settings to understand or manage such backups.

Sharing of information

Shadoku does not sell personal information or share gameplay information with advertisers, analytics providers, or other users. Platform-managed backup or transfer functionality, if enabled, is governed by the relevant platform provider's terms and privacy practices.

Data retention and deletion

Local gameplay data and preferences remain on your device until you reset the relevant information in the app or clear Shadoku's application storage through Android Settings. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local application data, although a previously created Android system backup may remain according to your device's backup settings and provider policies.

Shadoku does not offer user accounts, so there is no Shadoku account to delete.

Children's privacy

Shadoku does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game does not provide account registration, chat, advertising, or social sharing features.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Shadoku's functionality or data-handling practices change. The current version will be available at this webpage, with an updated effective date where appropriate.
